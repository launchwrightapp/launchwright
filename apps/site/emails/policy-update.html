A small update to our privacy policy.
If you're in the pilot, the launchwright team can now see your activity log and lesson progress. It helps us spot when you're stuck and fix problems faster.

What we see

What your AI tool did through launchwright (the tool, the repo, a short summary and whether it worked), and which lessons and practice exercises you've covered. You see the same log on your dashboard.

What we never see

Your code, your secret values, or your chats with your AI tool.
What stays the same
01 None of this is sent to an AI model.
02 Activity log entries are deleted after 12 months.
03 You can ask us to delete your data at any time.
We also corrected one line: we no longer match your GitHub account by the username in your application. The sign-in link in your "You're in" email, or your verified GitHub email, does that now.
