Questions?

Do I need to pay for an AI tool?

You use the AI tool you already have (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and others) on your own subscription. It just connects to Launchwright.

Where is my app hosted?

On your own accounts: GitHub for code, Cloudflare for hosting, and Neon or Supabase for the database. We never host your app, so it stays yours even if you leave Launchwright.

Are my credentials safe?

Tokens are encrypted and access is least-privilege. The AI never sees your raw credentials, and destructive actions need your approval in the dashboard.

Which frameworks are supported?

At launch: Next.js, Vite (React or Vue), Astro, and Hono. PHP/Laravel, Python, and Docker come with Level 2.

I'm a beginner. Can I do this?

Yes. Launchwright is built for beginners, students, career shifters, and people coming from vibe coding. The first time you meet a concept, you get the full explanation.
